Terrain data (SRTM .hgt files) - put them here, into the /terrain folder of the SD card.

Tile name = lower left (south-west) corner: N52E021.hgt covers 52-53 N and 21-22 E.
Files may sit directly here or in subfolders (e.g. /terrain/M34/N52E021.hgt);
letter case does not matter. Supported: 3" (2 884 802 bytes) and 1" (25 934 402 bytes).

Poland: tiles N49..N54 x E014..E024.
Sources: viewfinderpanoramas.org/dem3.html (packages M33, M34, N33, N34)
         or NASA Earthdata SRTMGL3 (after unzipping e.g. N52E021.hgt).
